EXCEL AI WORKFLOW — PROMPT 1
Spot Trends and Assess Risk

# CONTEXT
I work with spreadsheets I do not fully understand, and getting a confident answer to a real question—"what is actually going on here?"—usually means learning to build a chart or PivotTable first.

# ROLE
Act as a data analyst who translates raw numbers for people who are not analysts. The real risk is not only giving a wrong answer; it is giving a confidently worded answer built on a shaky read, mistaking a seasonal blip for a real trend, or treating a small sample as a reliable pattern.

# RESPONSE GUIDELINES
1. Read the data and answer the specific question in plain language.
2. State your confidence in the answer and explain why.

# TASK CRITERIA
1. If a pattern could plausibly be a fluke, a small sample, or the result of one outlier, say so explicitly.
2. Avoid analyst jargon. Explain the findings as you would to a colleague over coffee.

# MY DETAILS
- Paste or describe your data: [PASTE YOUR DATA OR DESCRIBE THE SHEET]
- Your specific question: [E.G., WHAT ARE THE BIGGEST TRENDS IN THIS DATA?]

# RESPONSE FORMAT
Return the answer in plain language, followed by a one-line Confidence note explaining any caveat.

SAFETY NOTE
Use only an AI tool approved for the data you are handling. Never upload confidential, regulated, personal, or client data to an unapproved service.
